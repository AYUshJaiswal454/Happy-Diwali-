पाँच दिन · The five nights

Tonight is the third of five.

  1. NIGHT 1

    Fri, 6 Nov

    Today

    धनतेरस

    Dhanteras

    Families buy something new in metal, a vessel, silver or gold, and light a lamp at the door for Yama, facing south.

  2. NIGHT 2

    Sat, 7 Nov

    Today

    छोटी दिवाली

    Choti Diwali

    Naraka Chaturdashi, for Krishna's victory over Narakasura. Many begin the day with an oil bath before sunrise.

  3. NIGHT 3

    Sun, 8 Nov

    Today

    लक्ष्मी पूजा

    Lakshmi Puja

    The new-moon night. Ayodhya lit its lamps to welcome Rama home; tonight homes light theirs to welcome Lakshmi.

  4. NIGHT 4

    Tue, 10 Nov

    Today

    गोवर्धन पूजा

    Govardhan Puja

    Krishna lifted Govardhan hill to shelter Braj from Indra's storm. Families offer annakut, a hill of cooked food.

  5. NIGHT 5

    Wed, 11 Nov

    Today

    भाई दूज

    Bhai Dooj

    Sisters put a tilak on their brothers' foreheads and pray for their long life. Brothers come bearing gifts.

Some calendars place Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj a day earlier, on 9 and 10 November.
